The 11th house is the most over-promised house in a modern reading. Labha means gain — and gain is not the same thing as free money 📈

In this deep-dive:
✦ Stated first — no yoga guarantees wealth, and the tradition never claimed one did
✦ Labha bhava defined: income, networks, fulfilment of desire
✦ Upachaya (a house that grows with effort and age) — and why that word changes everything
✦ The 11th as the 12th from the 12th, and as the 6th from the 6th
✦ Why the 11th and the 2nd are different questions: flow in vs what stays
✦ Reading order — 11th lord, 10th–11th and 6th–11th links, Jupiter, Ashtakavarga, dasha

A strong 11th on its own tells you very little. Read it with the 2nd, 6th and 12th 🙏
