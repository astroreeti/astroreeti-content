The D10 is the career chart. It is also the chart most often read backwards 📊

A varga is a divisional chart — each sign cut into equal parts, each part mapped to a full sign. The Dashamsha cuts every sign into ten parts of 3°, and classical texts assign it to karma: profession, position, public standing.

In this reel:
✦ How the D10 is actually computed — and the odd/even counting rule most hand-drawn D10s get wrong
✦ Why D1 comes first, always — a varga magnifies, it never overrules
✦ The three anchors read together: D10 lagna and its lord, the D1 10th lord, and the Amatyakaraka
✦ Amatyakaraka explained — the Jaimini chara karaka at the second-highest degree
✦ Refinements: 10th from the D10 Moon, dignity inside the varga, Ashtakavarga bindus, dasha for timing
✦ And the honest limit — the D10 names categories of work, never job titles

Agreement across all three anchors is the signal. One alone is noise 🙏
