Everyone says a house is "strong". The classical texts actually compute it 📐

Bala means strength. Bhava bala scores a house — not a planet — as a quantity you can compare against another house. It sits beside Shadbala, which scores the planets.

In this reel:
✦ Shadbala scores the actors, Bhava bala scores the stages
✦ Bhavadhipati bala — the house lord's own strength, the largest single input
✦ Bhava digbala — the directional component almost everyone drops
✦ Bhava drishti bala — net aspects on the house, which can come out negative
✦ Why a strong lord and an afflicted house are two separate measurements
✦ How to use the number — comparison and ranking, paired with Ashtakavarga
✦ And the caveat — texts and software compute it differently, so it is relative within one chart, never absolute

Bala says capacity. Dasha says when. They are not the same question 🙏
