Shadbala is six questions pretending to be one number — and the six ideas underneath it need no arithmetic at all 📐

In this deep-dive:
✦ Sthana bala — positional strength: dignity by sign and standing across the vargas
✦ Dig bala — directional strength, and the house each planet is at home in
✦ Kala bala — temporal strength: day/night, paksha, and the time lords
✦ Cheshta bala — motional strength, where retrograde motion scores HIGH (yes, really)
✦ Naisargika bala — the fixed brightness ranking, identical in every chart, and Drik bala — the net of every aspect falling on the planet
✦ Rupas and virupas, and why the real question is which planets met their own minimum

Strength is not benevolence. A strong malefic is strongly itself 🙏
