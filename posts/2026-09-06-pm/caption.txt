Anuradha — the nakshatra of Mitra, deity of friendship and of agreements kept 🪷

3°20′ to 16°40′ of Vrishchika, ruled by Shani, sitting in a sign owned by Mangal. Saturn's patience inside Mars's intensity — that pairing is the whole character.

In this reel:
✦ The name — anu-radha, "following Radha", the older name of Vishakha before it
✦ Why the sequence matters: ambition first, then the people you need to reach it
✦ Mitra, one of the Adityas, and why the Vedas almost always invoke him beside Varuna
✦ The symbols — a lotus that flowers in difficult water, and a triumphal archway a group passes through together
✦ What it's known for — devotion, friendship across distance, organising people, Deva gana
✦ And the shadow side tradition is honest about — loyalty held past the point where it serves anyone

Varuna holds cosmic law. Mitra holds the human bond that makes law liveable 🙏

Presented as the traditional Puranic and Vedic account, respectfully.
