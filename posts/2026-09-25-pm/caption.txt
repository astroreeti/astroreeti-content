A planet in its own sign or exaltation looks unambiguously strong. Most astrology apps stop right there. That's the gap.

Dignity is one of SIX components of Shadbala — not the total score:

✦ Sthana Bala — positional dignity (own sign, exaltation)
✦ Dig Bala — directional strength by house
✦ Kala Bala — time-based strength (day/night birth, paksha)
✦ Cheshta Bala — motional strength (retrograde actually scores high)
✦ Naisargika Bala — fixed natural strength per planet
✦ Drik Bala — aspect strength from benefics vs malefics

An exalted planet that's also combust loses Kala and Drik Bala fast — dignity says "strong," the full Shadbala score (measured in rupas, against a per-planet minimum) can say otherwise.

This is the exact cross-check a single-placement reading skips. And it's not the same tool as Ashtakavarga (benefic points across transits) or Bhava Bala (house strength) — three different questions.
