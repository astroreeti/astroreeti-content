Jupiter and Saturn are both 'good' planets. So why does it still feel like friction?

✦ Jupiter expands and says yes, Saturn limits and says not yet
✦ Look for a mahadasha (major period) of one with the antardasha (sub-period) of the other
✦ Also check if they aspect each other or the same house
✦ Treat the antardasha as a sprint: Jupiter sets vision, Saturn sets quality
✦ A lens for planning, never a prediction
