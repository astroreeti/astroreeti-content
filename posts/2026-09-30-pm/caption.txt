Part 4 of Dasha Seasons: Moon Dasha — the adaptability/EQ phase.

Sun Dasha asked you to be visible and hold authority. Moon Dasha asks something different: read the room, adjust in real time, manage the emotional temperature — not lead from the front.

✦ Success depends less on raw output, more on how well you sense what a situation actually needs
✦ The trap: over-adapting until you lose your own direction, mood swings reading as inconsistency to others, avoiding hard calls because you're managing everyone's feelings
✦ What works: use the EQ, don't get consumed by it — anchor people-reading skills to a plan that doesn't change with your mood
✦ Check your Antardasha (sub-period) within this Mahadasha — it tells you whether this season is for internal team management or external relationship-building
