Self-made wealth has a different house signature than inherited wealth — and it shows up as the same combination, again and again, across genuinely different charts.

✦ Self-made wealth leans on the 2nd (resources), 11th (gains), and 10th (effort) — earned, not received
✦ The recurring pattern: 2nd and 11th lords connected by conjunction, aspect, or exchange, with the 10th lord well-placed and linked into the same structure
✦ A kendra-trikona Raja Yoga reinforcing the 10th makes it stronger still
✦ It exists in the birth chart from day one but activates only in the Mahadasha/Antardasha of a participating lord — which is why the wealth looks sudden, decades in
✦ It can still be cancelled (yoga-bhanga) if a participating lord is debilitated or combust — the combination has to hold up, not just exist on paper
